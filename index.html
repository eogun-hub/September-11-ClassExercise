The Septeber 11 Attack

The Attack That Shocked America

Key events

  • September 11, 2001 — Four planes were hijacked.
  • Morning — Two planes struck the World Trade Center.
  • Later that morning — The Pentagon was struck, and another plane crashed in Pennsylvania.

The September 11 attacks (commonly known as 9/11) were a series of four coordinated airline hijackings and suicide attacks carried out by 19 militants associated with the extremist group al-Qaeda on September 11, 2001. It remains the deadliest terrorist attack on American soil in U.S. history, resulting in 2,977 victim fatalities. Both the North and South Towers of the World Trade Center collapsed within two hours of being hit due to structural damage and intense fires, altering the New York skyline and devastating lower Manhattan.

The events of September 11, 2001, affected people across the United States and around the world.

In remembrance of those who lost their lives on September 11, 2001.
